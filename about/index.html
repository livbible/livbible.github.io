About the LIV

Our goal is to create a Bible translation that is structurally and meaningfully close to the original manuscripts as possible while staying up to date with the English language.

To do this, we try to keep the original word count, order, and meaning as close as possible to our base manuscripts while still conforming to the rules of the current English language.We also use contractions to help achieve this.

The English language changes over time, but God's word never changes, this is why we need to regularly update the english Bible so that people can easily understand the Bible's message.

We use the Westminster Leningrad Codex & Textus Receptus manuscripts as our foundation. The Blue Letter Bible website, along with Strong's Concordance, is used to help understand the meaning of the manuscripts.

The LIV is currently a work in progress. You can support us, report issues/mistakes, or help translate a chapter to support the development of this project.
